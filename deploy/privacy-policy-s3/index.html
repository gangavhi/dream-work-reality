Privacy Policy

TrustNest (the “App”) · Last updated: May 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you use the TrustNest mobile application. We designed the App around on-device storage and your control over your household’s information.

Summary. Information you enter or import is processed and stored primarily on your device. We do not operate the App as a cloud vault for your documents or form answers: there is no TrustNest account backend in the App whose purpose is to upload your household records to our servers. If that changes in a future version, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent before new collection begins.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to you when you install and use TrustNest on a supported mobile device running iOS or Android (for example from the Apple App Store or Google Play). References to “we,” “us,” or “our” mean the developer and data controller for the App as shown on those store listings, including the legal entity name displayed there (see Contact).

2. Information the App may process

Depending on how you use the App, it may process:

  • Information you enter manually (for example, names, dates, addresses, or other fields you choose to save).
  • Documents and images you import (for example PDFs or photos you select via the system document picker).
  • Text derived on-device (for example text recognized from an imported document to help you organize information locally).
  • Technical diagnostics on the device that your operating system or device platform may collect separately under that platform’s terms—for example crash or diagnostics data if you opt in to sharing with Apple on iOS, with Google on Android, or similar options your device manufacturer may offer.

3. On-device storage and your control

The App is intended to keep your household information and related materials in an organized, local workspace on your device, so you are not repeatedly searching for the same documents when completing forms. You may update or delete information and imported materials within the App when those features are available, subject to limitations imposed by device backups, cloud backup services (for example iCloud, Google account backup, or your device manufacturer’s backup options where applicable), or mobile device management profiles.

4. Sharing — including proximity sharing

Where the App offers sharing, it is designed to be explicit and limited (for example, sharing only selected fields, with selected recipients, and only for a time window you choose). Features that use near-field communication (NFC) or similar proximity mechanisms are intended to transfer data directly between devices when you initiate an action, rather than routing that transfer through our servers by default. If a feature uses the internet (for example, to deliver an update), it will be described in the App and reflected here when applicable.

5. Third parties

  • Apple and Google. Distribution through the Apple App Store or Google Play, device services, and store-related processing are subject to each platform’s privacy policy and terms.
  • No advertising SDKs are described in the App’s platform disclosures at the time of this writing (for example the privacy manifest on iOS and the Play Data safety / store declarations on Android where applicable); if we add analytics or advertising, we will update this policy and each store’s privacy labels or declarations accordingly.

6. Security

You should protect your device with a passcode or biometric lock and keep your operating system updated. No mobile app can guarantee absolute security; you remain responsible for what you store on your device and who has physical access to it.

7. Children’s privacy

The App may allow you to store information about dependents. If you are a parent or guardian, you decide what to enter or import. The App is not directed to children to use on their own without appropriate supervision.

8. International users

Because data is stored on your device, processing occurs where you use the device. If you contact us, your message may be processed in the country where we operate support.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Material changes will be reflected in the App Store and Google Play listings where required.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests (including access, correction, or deletion where applicable), contact us at privacy@trustnest.app.
