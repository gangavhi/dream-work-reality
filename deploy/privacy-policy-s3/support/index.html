Getting started

  • Install TrustNest from the Apple App Store (iPhone or iPad) or Google Play (Android phone or tablet), depending on your device.
  • Keep your device on a supported version of iOS or Android as noted on the store listing you used.
  • Use the in-app flows to add household members, enter information manually, or import documents when those features are available in your build.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?
TrustNest is built around on-device storage so your household information stays under your control on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I update or remove information?
Use the app’s edit and delete actions for profiles, fields, and imported materials when offered in your version. Deleted items may still exist in device backups until those backups rotate.

I’m having trouble signing in or the app crashes.
Update to the latest TrustNest version, restart the device, and ensure you have enough free storage. If the problem continues, email support@trustnest.app with your device model, OS version (iOS or Android), and a short description of what happened (avoid sending sensitive documents by email).

Feature requests and feedback
We welcome suggestions at the same support address.

Store listings & billing

Apple App Store: Purchases, refunds, and subscription management are handled through Apple. For subscription or payment issues, use Apple’s report a problem flow or Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your device.

Google Play: Purchases, refunds, and subscription management are handled through Google Play. For subscription or payment issues, use the Play Store’s help options or Google Play’s subscriptions management on your device or account.

Security

If you believe you have found a security issue in TrustNest, contact us at support@trustnest.app with the subject line “Security” and enough detail for us to investigate. Please do not publicly disclose unresolved vulnerabilities before we have had a reasonable time to respond.
