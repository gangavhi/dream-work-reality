Privacy Policy

Trustnest (Android) · Effective date: May 14, 2026

Before you paste this page’s URL into Google Play: replace privacy@example.com everywhere below with a real contact address you monitor, then host this file on HTTPS (see repository instructions or your web host).

This policy describes how Trustnest (“the app”) handles information when you use the Android application published under package name com.dream.nestledger.dev. The app is designed to communicate with a backend service (API) that you or your organization operate or designate.

1. What the app does

The app uses the network to reach your configured API base URL. It may send and receive structured data (for example JSON) to support app features such as connectivity checks and in-app actions you trigger.

2. Information the app may process

  • Data you cause the app to send — When you use features that contact the backend, the app transmits request payloads required for those features (for example identifiers or labels you submit through the app’s flows).
  • Technical data — Like most networked apps, requests may include standard technical metadata handled by your servers and infrastructure (for example IP addresses, timestamps, or HTTP headers), depending on how your API and hosting are configured.
  • Data stored on the device — The app does not declare Android backup for app data in its manifest configuration shown in the project source; treat on-device state as local to the app unless you implement additional persistence.

3. How we use information

Information is used to operate the features you use, to communicate with your backend, and to respond to errors or connectivity issues. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Sharing

Data you send from the app is received by the backend and infrastructure you point the app at. We do not embed third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the app source as shipped in this repository; if that changes in a future version, this policy should be updated before release.

5. Security

Use HTTPS for production APIs. Network communication security also depends on your server configuration, certificates, and transport settings.

6. Children’s privacy

The app is not intended for children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
